## 3. `nvidia-cutlass-dsl` is discharged

**It is recorded as installed, and never as executed.**
`docs/bench-evidence/qwen35-4b-pinned-oracle-20260728.md:33` and

committed harness asserts it, and the only occurrence outside records is
`scripts/rc-sglang-oracle-lease.sh:227`, which greps a `pip freeze` for an
SGLang lease and gates nothing.

**It is not on any executed path this fleet can take.** Nineteen vLLM modules
import the package as `cutlass` at the pin. Three can be reached by the models
this project gates, and every one is gated behind device capability family 100,
which no gate host is — GB10 is `sm_121` and the workspace box `sm_120`, both
family 120; Thor is `sm_110`.

1. **NVFP4 linear.** `FlashInferCuteDslNvFp4LinearKernel.is_supported` returns
   `False, "FlashInfer cutedsl requires sm_10x"` unless
   `is_device_capability_family(100)`
   (`vllm/model_executor/kernels/linear/nvfp4/flashinfer.py:35-42` @
   `5559679229`). It is first in `_POSSIBLE_NVFP4_KERNELS`
   (`kernels/linear/__init__.py:448`) and is skipped, which is why §2.2's

2. **GDN prefill**, which matters because every Qwen3.5/3.6/3.8 arm on this gate

   @ `5559679229`; the docstring at `:97-100` says "opt-in only"). On a
   family-120 host neither branch fires and the resolver returns `triton`.
3. **FA4.** The `import cutlass` calls at
   `vllm/v1/attention/backends/flash_attn.py:1401-1403` and `:1489-1490` are
   function-local, on the FA4 path. The recorded oracle runs selected FA2:
   `docs/bench-evidence/qwen35-4b-pinned-oracle-20260728.md:35-36`, and

`scripts/dgx-online-serving.sh:506-508` records the parallel MXFP4 case, where

## 6. Why the re-measurement was not run

**Two reasons, and the second is the one that matters.**

**The fleet.** `rc devices` was read three times during this wave, each rc read
directly rather than through a pipe.

| Reading | `dgx:gpu0` | `thor:gpu0` | `strix:gpu0` | `orin:gpu0` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `busy`, another session | `busy` | `busy` | `ready`, `CUDA_AVAIL=False` |
| 2 | `busy` 29m56s | `busy` 2m26s | `ready` | `ready` |
| 3 | **`unhealthy (no contact 2h9m47s)`** | `busy` 36s | `ready` | `ready` |

At no reading was a device available that could serve either gate, and **the
third reading is worse than the first two**: `dgx:gpu0` has dropped out of
contact with the controller entirely. `orin:gpu0` reports `CUDA_AVAIL=False`
(`2026-09-02-db92053.md` §7.4) and `strix:gpu0` is AMD gfx1151, so neither is a
CUDA denominator host at all.

**`thor:gpu0` would not serve this even when it is free, and that is the point
rather than the scheduling.** Both affected gates are **GB10 `sm_121a`** gates.
Thor is `sm_110`. Every fact in §2.2 that puts FlashInfer on the denominator's
path is a capability-conditioned fact: the NVFP4 kernel selection resolves per
`has_device_capability(100)`, the autotune cache is keyed `.../121a/...`, and
the DFlash backend selection in §2.6 is recorded as "`flashinfer-native` fp8-KV
**sm121**". A number taken on Thor would be a different measurement on a
different device, not a re-measurement of
`docs/benchmarks/vllm-online-serving.md:66-67`, `:106-107` or
`docs/benchmarks/speculative-decoding.md:6`. Running one there and calling it a
step-6 baseline would be exactly the substitution
`docs/benchmarks/how-we-measure.md:56-57` forbids.

**The harness would refuse the run anyway, and that is a finding rather than an
excuse.** `FLASHINFER_VERSION` is read from the `parity-pin` block
(`serve_low_common.py:109`) and asserted at `online_gate.py:3552-3560` and again
at `:3714-3720`. At the target FlashInfer is `0.6.18`
(`2026-09-02-e126687.md` §3 and §5.2, `PIPLIST flashinfer-python 0.6.18`), so
`scripts/dgx-online-serving.sh` refuses at its `record-oracle` step
(`:305`). The `cutlass_source_tree` contract (§2.3) fails the same way, because
the tree it fingerprints lives inside the FlashInfer package that just moved.

So step 6's re-measurement and the harness's own fail-closed pin assertion are
in tension:

- Editing the `parity-pin` block first makes the measurement possible, but that
  edit **is** the pin advance, which step 6 is a precondition of. It is also
  outside this wave's authority and the spec's stop conditions.
- Measuring outside the committed harness produces exactly the kind of number
  this project refuses. `.agents/benchmark-record.md` and
  `docs/benchmarks/how-we-measure.md:56-57` are explicit that a
  stale-denominator or unasserted number is not published as binding, and #520
  exists because an oracle that "runs, looks healthy, and is not the one the
  record names" once produced 17 days of refused measurements.

Nothing in `.agents/upstream-sync.md` says which moves first. That is
`NEEDS_DECISION`, it is carried in §7, and this wave did not resolve it by
loosening an assertion.
